## Scope and method

This trace follows the numerical model, evaluation, explanation, robustness and scheduler claims published in the P13, P17, P20, P22 and P24 evidence documents to their saved row-level output or machine-readable summary, dataset partition, and model artifact/version. The source docs retain complete tables and per-engine/per-scenario records; this index avoids copying large outputs into another format.

The final P13 result contains per-engine truth/prediction/error rows. The P13-S1 prediction and P13-S2 pairing files are local ignored artifacts; their checksums and how to regenerate them are recorded in the tracked reports. The tracked P13 final report and frozen manifests provide a portable summary and model identity. P22's JSON result snapshots are tracked. P17/P20 ignored machine records, where used, are linked through reports that retain their hashes and regeneration commands. A missing ignored artifact in a fresh clone is not treated as evidence that a result can be regenerated without its source dataset and trusted model bundles.

## Limitations and next consumers

FD001 is simulated NASA C-MAPSS data. The final-test metrics describe 100 held-out simulated engines, not a real fleet. P22 validation and perturbation studies describe their saved development split. Scheduler quantities are assumptions; SHAP values are model attributions. Some row-level P13/P17/P20 source records are ignored local artifacts and require the source histories and verified bundles to regenerate; their report paths and checksums are retained here. P23 hosted checks were waived/unverified, so this trace does not assert that the same results are publicly deployed. P24-A3's candidate tag is `p24-rc1` on `bfcb1a4abc5abd4eff9fd4a11d7daf29b44f826d`; P24 gate #29 remains open.
